Vigil of the Isles Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Vigil of the Isles for Android (com.rclabs.vigiloftheisles) handles information. The game is published by RCLabs on Google Play.

Information the game uses

You can play without an account. The game does not collect or share personal or sensitive user data. It does not contain ads or analytics and does not use advertising identifiers.

The game uses Android's private app storage to remember your progress and settings. This includes your best run, selected character, earned gold, talent upgrades, and audio and control preferences. The game does not send this information to RCLabs or to any third party.

Keeping and deleting game data

Your saved progress and preferences stay in the game's private storage until you clear its storage in Android Settings or uninstall the game. Clearing storage deletes that copy of your progress. RCLabs does not maintain a server copy of your save.

Google Play

Google Play handles downloading and updating the game. Google's processing of information through Google Play is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

For questions about this policy or how the game handles information, contact RCLabs at rclabsgames@gmail.com.

If you email us, we use your email address and the information you choose to include to respond and follow up on your request. We keep this information as needed to handle the request and meet any applicable legal obligations. You can use the same address to ask us to delete your correspondence.
